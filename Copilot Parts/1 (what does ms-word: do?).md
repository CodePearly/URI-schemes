Microsoft Word, often called MS Word, is a widely used word processing software developed by Microsoft. Here are some of the key things you can do with it:

- **Create and Edit Documents**: Write, edit, and format text documents with ease.
- **Templates**: Use a variety of pre-made templates for resumes, reports, letters, and more.
- **Insert Elements**: Add images, tables, charts, hyperlinks, and other multimedia elements.
- **Styles and Formatting**: Customize fonts, colors, headings, and paragraph styles.
- **Collaboration**: Share documents with others for real-time collaboration and editing.
- **Review Tools**: Track changes, add comments, and use spell check and grammar tools.
- **Mail Merge**: Create personalized mass mailings by merging a document with a data source.
