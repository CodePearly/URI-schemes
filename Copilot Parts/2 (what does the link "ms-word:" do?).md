The "ms-word:" link is a custom URL scheme used by Microsoft Word to trigger specific actions within the Word application. For example, it can be used to open a specific document directly in Word, start a new document, or even go to a particular section within a document.

When you click on a link that starts with "ms-word:", it typically prompts your computer to open Microsoft Word and perform the action specified in the link. This can be handy for creating shortcuts or integrating Word with other applications or websites.
